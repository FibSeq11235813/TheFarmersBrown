Meet the Browns

Different strengths.
One very broad workshop.

We’re Tyler and Amanda Brown. Tyler gravitates toward building, troubleshooting, fabrication, and figuring out how things work. Amanda brings the growing operation, plants, food knowledge, and the eye for turning a pile of seedlings into something people can actually use.

The Farmers Brown is our way of putting those skills under one roof without pretending they’re all the same service. Build, Plant, and Grow are separate doors into the same company.

The rule: every new offering should clearly build something, plant something, or help something grow. If it can’t pass that test, it probably doesn’t belong here.
