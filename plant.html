Tampa vegetable seedlings & starter plants

Florida rewards
good timing.

Our planting seasons do not map neatly onto the rest of the country. Heat, humidity, rainfall, pest pressure, and short windows for some crops all matter. Plant is built around local timing and practical edible gardening rather than a generic nursery shelf.

If you already have containers, beds, or a tower, tell us what you’re growing in and how much sun you get. We can help narrow down a sensible seasonal mix.

Can I buy individual starter plants?

That is part of the Plant vision. Availability will vary with what is actively growing and ready to leave the nursery.

Can you choose plants for my season?

Yes. Seasonal recommendations are a core part of how Plant and Grow connect.

Can you install the plants for me?

Yes. For a more complete setup, ask about ready-to-grow beds or Garden-in-a-Day.

Do you only sell vegetables?

The focus is edible and useful gardening — vegetables, herbs and complementary plants — with the exact selection expanding over time.
