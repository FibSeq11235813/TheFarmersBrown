Garden consulting in Tampa Bay

When you don’t need
another product.

Sometimes the missing piece is knowing why the tomatoes stopped setting fruit, what to plant after summer, whether the bed gets enough sun, or how to automate watering without turning the yard into a plumbing experiment.

Grow is where those questions live. We can help you make a plan, troubleshoot an existing edible garden, or combine coaching with Build and Plant for a complete home-garden setup.

Can you set up the entire garden for me?

Yes. Garden-in-a-Day is designed as a complete Build + Plant + Grow service, with the exact scope quoted for your space.

Can I get help with a garden I already have?

Yes. Seasonal refreshes, plant replacement, troubleshooting and practical garden coaching fit under Grow.

Are nutrition services available now?

The site treats nutrition as a future Grow pathway rather than advertising a specific clinical or dietetic service before scope, availability, and applicable professional requirements are finalized.

Why is there a digital service here?

Because Grow is about helping useful things become more capable. It is intentionally experimental — and slightly hidden.
