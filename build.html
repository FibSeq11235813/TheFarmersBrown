Custom raised beds in Tampa Bay

Built for Florida gardens
and real people.

Raised garden beds in Tampa have to deal with heat, humidity, heavy rain, pests, and long growing seasons. We can size a bed around your space, preferred standing height, soil depth, access needs, and the crops you want to grow.

Our elevated garden beds are especially useful for people who would rather garden standing up than spend every harvest crouched on the ground.

Do you build custom sizes?

Yes. Custom sizing is one of the main reasons to work with Build. We can design around your footprint instead of forcing the footprint around a catalog product.

Do you deliver and install in Tampa Bay?

Local delivery and installation can be quoted based on the project, access, size, and location.

Can you fill and plant a raised bed too?

Yes. That is where Build connects with Plant and Grow. Ask about a ready-to-grow setup or Garden-in-a-Day.

Do you only build garden items?

No. Build can include useful tables, benches, shelving, garage organization and other practical woodworking that fits the Farmers Brown approach.
