> help --faq

Common
questions.

If your issue doesn't fit neatly into one of these boxes, that's fine. The form below is there for exactly that.

Do you only work with businesses?

No. Branch & Byte can help with personal computers and home technology as well as websites and small-business systems.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can work from an existing site, rebuild a dated one, or start fresh when that makes more sense.

Do you offer SEO?

Website projects can include practical SEO foundations such as page titles, descriptions, heading structure, internal linking, structured data, local search language, sitemap and robots configuration. Larger ongoing SEO campaigns can be scoped separately.

What computer problems can I send?

If you're not sure, send the symptoms. Slow performance, upgrade questions, setup problems, component issues, connectivity headaches, and general troubleshooting are all reasonable starting points.

Where do you work?

Branch & Byte is based in the Tampa Bay area. Some website and digital work can be handled remotely; physical repair or setup depends on the job and location.
