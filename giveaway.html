No purchase will be necessary to participate in the live sweepstakes. The final free entry method will be described in the Official Rules.

No. Current Farmers Brown purchases do not earn giveaway entries. If promotional purchase-related entries are used later, that will begin only under published Official Rules.

The geographic area, minimum age, exclusions, and other eligibility requirements are still being finalized and will be stated before launch.
