> explain local_seo

Local SEO without
writing for robots.

For Tampa Bay businesses, we build the signals search engines need while keeping the page readable for actual people. That can include service-specific titles and descriptions, useful location language, structured data, internal links, crawlable navigation, sitemap configuration and fast responsive pages.

That helps a Tampa-area company establish relevance for searches such as “Tampa Bay web design,” “Tampa website designer,” or the specific service and neighborhood combinations its customers use. No legitimate web designer can guarantee a particular Google ranking, but the site should at least give search engines a clear, technically sound page to rank.

Projects can include local SEO foundations such as titles and descriptions, semantic heading structure, internal links, structured data, sitemap and robots configuration, mobile performance and locally relevant service copy.
