questions.

What computer problems do you work on?

Slow or unstable computers, upgrade questions, new PC setup, file migration, common hardware and software troubleshooting, and everyday connectivity or peripheral problems are all reasonable starting points.

Do you work on laptops and desktops?

Yes, for general troubleshooting and practical upgrades. If a job requires specialized board-level or manufacturer-specific equipment, we’ll discuss that before proceeding.

Can you tell me if an older computer is worth upgrading?

Yes. We can compare the current hardware, intended use and likely upgrade cost before you spend money on a machine that may be better replaced.
