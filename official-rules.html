Design principles

What we already
intend to preserve.

  • No purchase necessary. The live promotion will include a no-purchase path to enter.
  • Clear disclosure. We will distinguish marketing, purchase activity, launch-alert signup, and actual sweepstakes entry.
  • Published terms first. Entries will not open until the final rules and entry mechanics are public.
  • Documented winner selection. Eligible entries will be handled under one stated process.

Important notice

This pre-launch page is intentionally conservative: it does not create entry rights, promise an entry multiplier, set an ARV, or claim eligibility in any location.
