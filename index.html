Meet the Browns

Built by Tyler.
Grown by Amanda.

We’re Tyler and Amanda Brown. The Farmers Brown grew out of our own garden, our own projects, and a habit of making the thing we wished already existed.

Tyler brings the building, troubleshooting, fabrication, and general compulsion to figure things out. Amanda brings the plants, garden planning, usability, aesthetics, food and nutrition background, and an equally dangerous supply of new ideas.

The point of Build. Plant. Grow. isn’t to become three unrelated businesses. It’s to give one flexible family business room to follow useful work wherever it naturally leads.
